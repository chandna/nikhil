WORK — 03 CASE STUDIES · 2022—2026

THE CONTEXT

Sales Navigator is LinkedIn’s enterprise tool used by sellers to find and engage with prospects. With the core job to get a meeting with prospects, many sellers make cold reach-out (calls, emails, messages, connection requests) to prospects and suffer from low response rates.   77% of enterprise sellers see 2× more effectiveness over cold outreach when they asked a mutual connection (aka warm path) for help, yet most still hesitated to ask.

The reason: low trust in connection quality, manual work and cognitive load, and a sub-optimal user experience. The had an intent, but dissuaded due to a low confidence and high effort.

What I designed

  • Designed a framework that reduces friction and extends across the seller journey
  • Designed to build trust in the system with a weighted ranking system prioritizing shared work & education history
  • An explainability layer giving sellers the language to act
  • AI-powered messaging for seller to reach out to introducers
  • Introducer experience with a native 3-way conversation facilitation inside the Flagship LinkedIn app

HOW DID THE SELLERS REACT

"Of the 10 leads I had a warm intro with where my connection shared a past work experience, I booked meetings with 80% of the leads."

Impact

≥2×conversion over cold outreach, reported by enterprise sellers
77%CONVERSIONS LIFT IN GETTING A MEETING
18%LIFT IN WAU ENGAGEMENT WITH WARM CONNECTION PATHS

The problem

The gap between a design decision and a shipped pixel was measured in weeks and meetings. Designers had no way to touch production code without an engineering handoff.

What I built

Design Systems Playground: a cloned, static library of every production UI component, icon, pattern and coding standard from Sales Navigator's codebase.  Agent pipeline: 5 custom AI agents using the Playground as their source of truth:

  • Welcome · Investigator · Builder · Code Review · PR
  • Resolve Jira tickets & write production-grade Ember.js
  • Open pull requests on LinkedIn's codebase — no engineer required

Impact

8NON-CODER DESIGNERS SHIPPED TO PRDDUTION INDEPENDENTLY
20+PR MERGED IN 2 MONTHS
weeks → hoursdesign-to-deployment, compressed

Why it matters

The team's velocity is no longer bounded by engineering availability. Experimentation and front end bug fixes were done faster. It gave designers more control over their work as there is no translation loss or engineering hand-off.

THE CONTEXT

The vision of LinkedIn Groups was to have every member of the global workforce feel a sense of belonging with their professional communities. However, groups struggled with the problem of low engagement. Only 2500 of 100,000 groups were healthy. Groups organizers (admins) didn't have the required tools to drive engagement, manage group membership and fight spam.

What I designed

  • End-to-end group organizer vision:  Creation → Onboarding → Management → Recognition
  • Automated review of join requests from new members
  • Automated content moderation
  • Automated welcome note for new members
  • Recognition for successful group management

Impact

+61%HEALTHY GROUPS
+71%ENGAGED NEW MEMBERS
↓4×SPAM CONTENT

The insight

The automation didn't just save time — it improved community quality. Removing overhead let admins act on the requests that actually mattered.
